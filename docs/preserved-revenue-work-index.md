# Preserved Revenue Work Index

## Purpose

Earlier EPOCH prototype work produced useful revenue, education, marketing, and
automation planning. The boundary correction does not discard that work. It
changes ownership: WORKSHOP owns the active revenue/product material, and EPOCH
keeps only the schedule-bound pieces needed for appointments, deadlines,
reminders, availability, and customer-safe schedule status.

## Preserve under WORKSHOP

WORKSHOP should carry forward these useful prior concepts:

- EIKEN and broader English/test-prep offers across EIKEN 5 through 1, TOEIC,
  IELTS, TOEFL, school writing, university writing, business English, and
  professional communication.
- Submission-first delivery, including async correction packs, diagnostic
  submissions, review queues, before/after evidence, and reusable feedback
  templates.
- Cohorts and premium live sessions when they improve conversion or outcomes,
  while avoiding a labor-heavy classroom default.
- Subscription materials, study strategies, worksheets, templates, and
  self-serve progress assets.
- Marketing and advertising plans for Japan-wide and later global reach,
  including careful under-19 eligibility and compatibility gates.
- Non-education revenue lanes: programming, web/app builds, automation,
  database/CRM/management systems, tech support, clerical/admin execution, and
  practical operations consulting.
- ARA-assisted revenue production through MONITOR, SYMBIOSIS, FURYOKU, and
  other CITADEL projects, with human approval where customer-facing or legally
  sensitive.

## Preserve through EPOCH collaboration

When those offers need time-bound coordination, WORKSHOP should request EPOCH
support for:

- appointments and paid session times
- cohort calendars
- submission and review deadlines
- reminders and follow-up windows
- customer-safe schedule/status updates
- overdue schedule risk

EPOCH should not own the package, price, curriculum, sales funnel, or delivery
pipeline simply because the work has a date on it.

## MONITOR placement

WORKSHOP MONITOR should use revenue/delivery labels:

- Revenue Completion
- Revenue Scorecard
- Revenue Timeline
- Revenue Audit
- Revenue Receipts
- Delivery Log
- Revenue Search
- Offer Template

EPOCH MONITOR should use schedule/calendar labels:

- Schedule Completion
- Schedule Scorecard
- Schedule Timeline
- Schedule Audit
- Schedule Receipts
- Scheduler Log
- Calendar Search
- Schedule Template

The two monitors may exchange schedule/status handoff records, but neither
monitor should make the projects look like one merged product bench.
