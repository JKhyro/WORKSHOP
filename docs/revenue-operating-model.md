# Revenue Operating Model

## First operating lanes

1. Submission-based English writing/test-prep review.
2. Small cohorts and premium live sessions only where they materially improve
   conversion or outcomes.
3. Subscription materials and strategy access after repeated demand is proven.
4. Consulting, tech support, admin/database/CRM, and automation services as
   parallel revenue lanes.
5. ARA-assisted service production with minimal manual intervention.

## Labor rule

Prefer delivery that scales:

- submissions over live classes
- cohorts over 1:1 where the buyer goal clusters
- templates and reusable materials over custom one-off explanations
- automation over repeated clerical work
- schedule/status self-service over manual update messages

## Operating workflow

Every sellable lane should have explicit WORKSHOP-side readiness records:

- package eligibility before customer-facing offer display
- compatibility routing before accepting under-19 or high-touch work
- submission review cycles before materials enter delivery
- cohort or subscription plans before scheduling group time

EPOCH may receive timing handoffs after WORKSHOP clears intake, but EPOCH does
not own the package, customer, submission, cohort, price, or delivery record.

## Education lane

WORKSHOP can offer EIKEN 5 through 1, TOEIC, IELTS, TOEFL, school writing,
university writing, business English, and professional communication services.

Students under 19 should require compatibility assessment and guardian-aware
terms or be priced as higher-touch work.

Default package flow should stay lower-labor where possible:

- submission packs open first when the material is ready
- cohorts open after compatible demand clusters
- materials subscriptions can stay available without forcing a live class
- under-19 routes stay compatibility-gated and non-default

## Non-education lanes

WORKSHOP should also track revenue opportunities for:

- programming and web/app builds
- automation and internal tools
- tech support
- clerical/admin execution
- database/CRM/management-system setup
- business consulting and operations consulting where legally and practically
  appropriate

## Pricing posture

Do not use founder-pricing language by default. Present the business as a
professional operating service. Discounts, pilots, or diagnostics can exist,
but they should be framed as specific package terms, not as insecurity about
the business being new.
